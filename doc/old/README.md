# Historical documentation archive

These pages record earlier Sandbar designs and interfaces. They are **superseded**, are outside the normal onboarding path, and do not establish 0.2.0 behavior. Begin with the [Sandbar README](../../README.md) or [current quick start](../guides/quickstart.md).

Most pages retain their technical examples with an editorial notice and current destinations. Historical commands, identifiers, counts, and response shapes have not been revalidated. The early MCP and task pages are shorter historical dispositions: their obsolete wire examples and implementation-stage narratives are retired, while their design questions remain visible. The original texts remain in version history.
